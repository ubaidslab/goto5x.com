# goto5x.com — Terms of Service / Seller Agreement (Draft)

**Last updated:** [date] · **Version:** draft-2

## 1. What goto5x.com Is
**goto5x.com ("the Platform") is a facilitation workspace, not a marketplace
operator or the seller of record for anything sold through it.** It lets
independent merchants ("Sellers") create and operate their own online stores,
and connects them to third-party suppliers ("Suppliers") who may fulfill
orders directly. **The Platform never collects, holds, or transmits payment
for any sale** — a Buyer pays a Seller directly, by whichever method (bank
transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or Cash on Delivery) that Seller has
configured for their own store. Each Seller bears full and sole
responsibility for their own products, listings, pricing, payment
collection, order fulfillment, customer service, and compliance with
applicable law; Sellers remain responsible for any Supplier listing they
approve into their store.

## 2. Accounts and the Seller Agreement
- **Sellers** and **Suppliers** must register an account and may be required
  to complete identity verification (KYC) before certain features are
  enabled.
- **Every Seller must accept the current version of this Seller Agreement at
  signup**, and again whenever a material version is published — the
  Platform records the accepting timestamp and IP address. A Seller who has
  not accepted the current version cannot use the dashboard beyond the
  acceptance prompt itself.
- **Buyers** may check out as guests. Order status is available via a secure
  link sent to the email address provided at checkout — no Buyer account is
  required.
- Account holders are responsible for the accuracy of the information they
  provide and for maintaining the security of their credentials.

## 3. Payment Collection and Platform Fees
- **Direct Seller Collection.** The Platform is never a party to the payment
  for any order. A Seller configures the payment method(s) their storefront
  accepts (bank account details, JazzCash number, Easypaisa number, and/or
  Cash on Delivery) and those instructions are shown to the Buyer once an
  order is placed. Payment happens entirely between Buyer and Seller,
  outside the Platform.
- **Platform commission.** The Platform charges a commission of **1% of the
  order subtotal** (product + shipping, net of any discount applied) on
  every order the Seller confirms as paid. [Confirm final published rate and
  whether it varies by plan/category before publishing — see `docs/SRS.md`
  §5.6c, §8.3.] Commission accrues to a **monthly invoice** the Platform
  sends the Seller; the Seller is responsible for settling that invoice by
  the stated due date via the payment method the Platform specifies.
- **Non-payment.** An invoice left unpaid past the stated grace period
  results in the Seller's store being **automatically suspended** —
  storefront offline to new Buyers, though the Seller can still fulfill any
  order already placed — until the invoice is settled.
- **Honest reporting is a condition of using the Platform.** Because the
  Platform never holds or observes payment for a sale, it relies on each
  Seller accurately marking their own orders paid or cancelled. Deliberately
  failing to report a completed sale, or falsely marking an order cancelled,
  to avoid owing commission is a material breach of this Agreement (see
  §6-7).

## 4. Supplier Listings
- Suppliers submit product listings, which each Seller must explicitly review and
  approve before they appear in that Seller's store — the Platform does not publish
  a Supplier's listing on a Seller's behalf.
- Listings must accurately disclose shipping cost, estimated delivery time, and the
  countries the Supplier can deliver to. Orders will not be accepted for a
  destination the listed Supplier cannot serve.
- The Platform may remove a listing, suspend a Supplier, or suspend a Seller's store
  where a listing is found to violate applicable law or Platform policy (including
  prohibited or counterfeit goods).

## 6. Enforcement Ladder
Depending on the severity and pattern of a violation, the Platform may apply,
at its discretion and in any order it judges appropriate: a **warning**; an
**account restriction** (e.g. blocking new listings or new orders while the
account otherwise remains active); a **suspension** (store taken offline,
in-flight orders remain fulfillable so existing Buyers are not stranded); or
a **permanent ban**. Enforcement decisions are made by Platform staff, not
automated, and are recorded internally for accountability.

## 7. Suspension and Termination
The Platform may suspend or terminate a Seller's or Supplier's account for breach of
this Agreement, fraud, or legal risk, with in-flight orders handled as described in the
Platform's operational policies so existing Buyers are not left unserved wherever
reasonably possible.
