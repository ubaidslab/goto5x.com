## 1. What goto5x.com Is
goto5x.com ("the Platform") is a service that lets independent merchants ("Sellers")
create and operate their own online stores. The Platform is not the seller of record
for products sold through Seller stores — each Seller is responsible for their own
products, listings, pricing, customer service, and compliance with applicable law.
Some products may be sourced from third-party suppliers ("Suppliers") who fulfill
orders directly; Sellers remain responsible for the listings they approve into their
store.

## 2. Accounts
- **Sellers** and **Suppliers** must register an account and may be required to
  complete identity verification (KYC) before certain features (e.g. payout
  eligibility, reduced holds) are enabled.
- **Buyers** may check out as guests. Order status is available via a secure link
  sent to the email address provided at checkout — no Buyer account is required.
- Account holders are responsible for the accuracy of the information they provide
  and for maintaining the security of their credentials.

## 3. Fees, Commission, and Payouts
- The Platform charges a commission of **3% of the order subtotal** (product +
  shipping, net of any discount applied), deducted automatically at the time of
  sale. [Confirm final published rate and whether it varies by plan/category before

- Funds from a Seller's sales are credited to an internal ledger and become
  available for payout after a **holding period of up to 22 days** per transaction.
  This hold exists to protect against disputes, chargebacks, and fraud, and may be
  shortened for Sellers who meet the Platform's trust criteria over time.
- The Platform may apply an additional **rolling reserve** — a percentage of sales
  held back beyond the standard hold period — to a Seller's account where risk
  factors warrant it (e.g. dispute rate, account age, irregular order activity).
  Reserved funds are released after a defined period if no dispute arises.
- Payouts are requested by the Seller against their available balance and are
  reviewed before disbursement. The Platform may decline, delay, or freeze a payout
  where fraud, policy violation, or an open dispute is reasonably suspected.

## 4. Supplier Listings
- Suppliers submit product listings, which each Seller must explicitly review and
  approve before they appear in that Seller's store — the Platform does not publish
  a Supplier's listing on a Seller's behalf.
- Listings must accurately disclose shipping cost, estimated delivery time, and the
  countries the Supplier can deliver to. Orders will not be accepted for a
  destination the listed Supplier cannot serve.
- The Platform may remove a listing, suspend a Supplier, or suspend a Seller's store
  where a listing is found to violate applicable law or Platform policy (including
  prohibited or counterfeit goods).
