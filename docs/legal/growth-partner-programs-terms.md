# uzeyn.com — Growth & Partner Programs Terms (Draft)

**Last updated:** [date] · **Version:** draft-1 · **Covers:** Certified
Ambassador, Student Referral, Creator programs (Careers has its own terms,
built separately)

## 1. No Self-Serve Enrollment, No Guarantee of Approval
Participation in any Growth & Partner Program (Ambassador, Student
Referral, Creator) requires an application. **Meeting the published
eligibility criteria for a program means you may apply — it does not mean
your application will be approved.** uzeyn.com reviews every application
manually and may reject an application at its sole discretion, including
for reasons not captured by the automated eligibility check.

## 2. Program Terms

### 2.1 Certified Ambassador
- Requires an eligible paid plan at the time of application (the current
  list of eligible plans is available on request).
- Referral commission: currently 8% (subject to change) of a referred
  seller's own paid plan-subscription payments, for the first 6 months
  (subject to change) following that seller's attribution to you. This
  commission window is fixed at the moment your referral is recorded and
  does not change even if the published rate/window later changes.
- Referral commission is calculated **only** against a referred seller's
  plan-subscription payments to uzeyn.com. It is never calculated against
  that seller's own storefront sales, revenue, or wallet top-ups.
- A monthly performance reward (a free premium plan period, or an
  equivalent credit) may be granted for referring a sufficient number of
  new paid subscriptions within a calendar month — the current threshold
  is available on request and subject to change.
- Certificate tiers (e.g. Silver/Gold/Diamond) are unlocked by lifetime
  referral performance against published thresholds, which are subject to
  change.

### 2.2 Student Referral
- Referral commission: currently 5% (subject to change) of a referred
  seller's own paid plan-subscription payments, for the first 3 months
  (subject to change) following attribution.
- Same "plan-subscription payments only, never GMV/sales/top-ups" rule as
  2.1 above.

### 2.3 Creator
- Same referral-commission terms as Student Referral (2.2).
- An additional view-based reward may be available for qualifying
  promotional content on supported platforms. **A reported view count is
  never itself sufficient for a reward.** Every submission is reviewed
  manually against posting guidelines before any reward is calculated, and
  total monthly reward per Creator is capped (the current cap is available
  on request and subject to change). An unverified or rejected submission
  earns nothing.

## 3. One Referral Source Per Referred Seller
A referred seller may only ever be attributed to a single referrer, across
every program, determined by whichever valid referral was recorded first.
A later referral attempt for the same seller — through any program — has
no effect.

## 4. Referred Seller: Plan-Fee Payments Are Non-Refundable
This section applies to you if you sign up for a uzeyn.com seller account
using someone else's referral link or code — not to the referrer earning
commission under §2 above.

**If your seller account is attributed to any Growth & Partner Program
referral (Ambassador, Student Referral, or Creator), your plan-subscription
payments become final and non-refundable from the moment that attribution
is recorded, including the standard first-cycle refund window otherwise
available to sellers who sign up without a referral.** This applies
regardless of whether the referral ultimately qualifies the referrer for
any commission.

You should understand this before completing signup with a referral link
or code — it cannot be waived or reversed after the fact by contacting
support.

## 5. Withdrawals
Earnings post to your existing uzeyn.com wallet. You may request a
withdrawal once your balance meets the published minimum. Every withdrawal
request is reviewed by uzeyn.com before any funds are disbursed; a
rejected request does not affect your wallet balance.

## 6. Fraud, Clawback, and Suspension/Termination
uzeyn.com monitors for self-referral and fraudulent referral patterns. A
confirmed finding may result in cancellation of pending rewards, a
clawback of previously credited or withdrawn amounts (which may take your
wallet balance negative), suspension, or termination of your program
participation, at uzeyn.com's discretion — with or without prior notice,
for Terms violation, fraud, or suspicious activity. uzeyn.com may suspend
or terminate any approved participant's access, in-flight rewards, or
account at any time for these reasons.

## 7. Changes
Commission rates, windows, thresholds, caps, and eligibility criteria
published for these programs may change at any time; changes apply to
future activity only, except where this document states an amount is
locked in at the time of a specific event (e.g. §2.1/§2.2's commission
window).

## 8. Earn Your Plan (Draft Terms — 2026-10-08)

Replaces the discontinued Student Referral program (§2.2) as the
general seller-referral-incentive mechanism. Not available at global
launch — opens post-launch once a minimum number of paying sellers
exist on the platform, for a limited enrollment window.

### 8.1 No Purchase Required
Participation in Earn Your Plan requires no purchase, payment, or
existing paid subscription — a seller on the Free tier may enroll and
refer.

### 8.2 What Counts as a Qualifying Referral
A referral counts toward your rungs (§8.3) only once **all** of the
following are true of the referred account: it is a genuinely new
account (not a returning or previously-attributed seller); it has
purchased a paid subscription; it has used and paid for one complete
billing cycle; the standard 14-day refund window on that payment has
passed with no refund issued; and there is no open payment dispute or
chargeback on that payment. **A free-tier signup alone never counts,
under any circumstance.**

### 8.3 Rungs and Rewards
Published rungs, reward amounts, and qualification windows are
available on the Earn Your Plan program page at the time of your
enrollment and are **subject to change for future enrollment periods**
— your own enrollment's rungs and windows are fixed at the moment you
enroll and do not change retroactively. Rewards are account
credit/entitlement only — **never cash**, never transferable to another
account or person, and may be clawed back if the qualifying referral is
later reversed (refund or chargeback).

### 8.4 One Program Per Referred Account
A referred account may be attributed to only one referral source across
every UZEYN growth program (Earn Your Plan or UZEYN Partners, §9),
determined by the first valid referral link used. Self-referral
(enrolling and then referring your own second account) is prohibited
and will be treated as fraud under §6.

### 8.5 No Income Guarantee
Earn Your Plan is a discretionary incentive program, not an investment,
a business opportunity, or a guaranteed-earnings arrangement. uzeyn.com
makes no representation about how many referrals any participant will
achieve.

## 9. UZEYN Partners (Draft Terms — 2026-10-08)

An invite-only, application-based recognition and referral program,
structurally separate from Earn Your Plan (§8). Conceptually replaces
the discontinued Certified Ambassador program (§2.1) but is not that
program repriced — different eligibility, different benefits, different
mechanics.

### 9.1 Invite-Only, Application-Based, No Purchase Required
UZEYN Partners status is granted by application and manual UZEYN
review, never purchased and never automatically earned by meeting a
threshold alone. **No purchase of any kind is required to apply or to
participate.**

### 9.2 Single-Level Program — No Recruitment Reward
UZEYN Partners is a single-level referral program. **A Partner earns
nothing for recruiting, referring, or onboarding another Partner.**
Every benefit described in this section is earned solely from your own
direct referrals of new, paying UZEYN sellers — never from any other
Partner's activity.

### 9.3 Non-Cash Benefits
Subject to published eligibility criteria (available on the Partners
program page, subject to change for future applicants): a verification
certificate; a storefront badge; an opt-in public directory listing (no
private information is ever shown without your separate, explicit
consent); free access to a published plan tier while your account
remains active and in good standing under the then-current eligibility
rule; a community-admin designation where applicable; access to partner
meetups where offered.

### 9.4 Referral Bonus
A Partner may earn a referral bonus calculated as a published
percentage of the cleared net revenue (after applicable tax and
third-party payment-processing fees) collected from a referred
customer's subscription payments, for a published duration following
that customer's attribution to you. **The current rate and duration are
available on the Partners program page and are subject to change for
future referrals** — a specific referral's rate and duration are fixed
at the moment that referral is recorded and do not change
retroactively. A payment counts toward your bonus only once it meets
the same qualifying-referral standard as §8.2 above. The bonus is
released only after your identity has been verified and you have
completed a scheduled onboarding call — not before. Payout method,
currency, and minimum payout threshold are published on the Partners
program page and are subject to change; payouts are issued manually,
at uzeyn.com's discretion as to timing within any published service
window.

### 9.5 No Income Guarantee; No Special Payment Gateways
UZEYN Partners is a discretionary recognition and referral program, not
an investment, a business opportunity, or a guaranteed-earnings
arrangement — uzeyn.com makes no representation about achievable
earnings. Partner status does not grant access to any payment gateway,
processing arrangement, or financial product beyond what is generally
available to any UZEYN seller.

### 9.6 Identity Verification
Application requires a manual UZEYN review of your identity and payout
details before activation. Any identity document submitted for this
purpose is used solely for verification, is not retained beyond what is
necessary to complete that verification, and is handled under
uzeyn.com's standard data-protection practices.

### 9.7 Affiliate Disclosure Obligation
If you promote UZEYN as a Partner — in any content, post, video, or
communication where you stand to earn a referral bonus under §9.4 — you
must clearly and conspicuously disclose that relationship to your
audience, consistent with applicable consumer-protection and
advertising-disclosure law. Failure to disclose is a Terms violation
under §6.

### 9.8 Fraud, Clawback, Suspension, and Termination
§6 above (Fraud, Clawback, and Suspension/Termination) applies in full
to UZEYN Partners participation, including the referral bonus under
§9.4.
