# Teams & Community Sponsorship

## What it does

Lets an eligible seller ("leader") sponsor other sellers onto a shared plan
tier — a group discount / community-building mechanism, not a shared store.

## How it works

- A leader (on a Team-leader-eligible individual plan tier) creates a team
  and picks one of three Team tiers, each with its own flat per-seat price.
- Invited members join as sponsored seats; while sponsored, a member's own
  plan becomes whatever the leader's Team tier grants — the same feature set
  for every seat, never a per-member-chosen price.
- The leader is billed monthly for (active sponsored seats × that tier's
  seat price) straight from their own wallet.
- A member who leaves (or is dropped for non-payment) gracefully downgrades
  to the Free plan at their next cycle — never an abrupt cutoff mid-cycle.

## Settings keys

| Key | What it tunes |
|---|---|
| `teams.leader_eligible` | Which individual plan tiers may create a team |
